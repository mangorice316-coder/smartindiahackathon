# Authoritative Source Attribution & Licensing Register

**Project**: AI-Powered Landslide Risk Intelligence & Early Warning System (LRIDS)  
**Governance Protocol**: NDSAP (India), ODbL 1.0 (OSM), and Copernicus Open Access

---

## 1. Primary Government Scientific Sources (Tier 1 & Tier 2)

### 1.1 Geological Survey of India (GSI)
- **Datasets**: National Landslide Forecasting Centre (NLFC) Validated Inventory, National Landslide Susceptibility Mapping (NLSM) 8 Core Factors.
- **Provider**: Geological Survey of India, Ministry of Mines, Government of India.
- **Access Endpoints**:
  - `https://nlfc.gsi.gov.in/`
  - `https://bhukosh.gsi.gov.in/`
- **Licensing**: National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP), Government of India. Open for civil defense, research, and non-commercial disaster risk mitigation.
- **Attribution Statement**:
  > *"Geological data, landslide susceptibility maps, and ground-truth inventory courtesy of the Geological Survey of India (GSI), Ministry of Mines, Government of India."*

### 1.2 ISRO / NRSC (National Remote Sensing Centre)
- **Datasets**: Landslide Atlas of India (1998–2022), Bhuvan Landslide Geoportal.
- **Provider**: National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC), Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), Department of Space, Government of India.
- **Access Endpoint**: `https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/`
- **Licensing**: ISRO Data Policy / Bhuvan Open Services.
- **Attribution Statement**:
  > *"Historical landslide scars, event boundaries, and route vulnerability layers courtesy of the National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC), ISRO, Department of Space, Government of India."*

### 1.3 India Meteorological Department (IMD)
- **Datasets**: High-Resolution 0.25° Gridded Daily Precipitation, Automated Weather Station (AWS) Mesonet Streams.
- **Provider**: India Meteorological Department, Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India.
- **Access Endpoints**:
  - `https://mausam.imd.gov.in/`
  - `https://data.gov.in/`
- **Licensing**: Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India / NDSAP.
- **Attribution Statement**:
  > *"Precipitation observations, hourly rainfall intensity, and antecedent meteorological records courtesy of the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Ministry of Earth Sciences."*

## 2. International Remote Sensing Sources (Tier 2)

### 2.1 European Space Agency (ESA) / Copernicus Programme
- **Datasets**: Sentinel-1 C-Band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR), Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI).
- **Provider**: European Union / European Space Agency (ESA).
- **Access Endpoint**: `https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/`
- **Licensing**: Copernicus Open Access Policy (CC-BY 4.0 compatible).
- **Attribution Statement**:
  > *"Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2014–2024), processed by the LRIDS Geospatial Pipeline."*

### 2.2 NASA / USGS
- **Datasets**: Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) 1 Arc-Second 30m Global DEM.
- **Provider**: NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory / USGS Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center.
- **Access Endpoint**: `https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/`
- **Licensing**: Public Domain / NASA Open Data Policy.
